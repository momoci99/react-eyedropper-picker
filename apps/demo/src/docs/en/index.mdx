# react-eyedropper-picker

A React color picker that is fun to pick with. It uses the browser's own eye dropper where there is one, and a pixel-accurate loupe for images in every browser, mobile included.

<Example code={imagePickerCode}>
  <ImagePicker />
</Example>

Move over the image: the loupe shows each pixel, the wheel zooms, a click picks. Sound is on in this example: after your first click it ticks as you cross pixels.

## What you get

- **`ColorPicker`**: saturation panel, hue and opacity sliders, hex input, recent colors, light and dark themes. Keyboard and touch friendly.
- **Native eye dropper**: the [EyeDropper API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/EyeDropper_API) picks from anywhere on screen in Chromium browsers.
- **Loupe** (`react-eyedropper-picker/loupe`): pick from an `<img>`, `<canvas>` or `<video>` in every browser, with free zoom, grid, sampling and touch support.
- **Feel**: optional tick and click sounds, haptics, smooth tracking and animations that respect reduced motion.
- **Small and safe**: no runtime dependencies, typed, SSR-safe. The loupe lives in its own entry, so the picker alone stays small.
